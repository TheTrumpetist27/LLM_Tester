the Blue Mountains Botanic Garden Mount Tomah is one of the most beautiful gardens in the greater Sydney region, if not the world . the notion of botanic gardens has evolved and become more nuanced over the years, with their roles changing over time . there are officially 1,775 botanic gardens in 148 countries worldwide, including more than 130 in australia and new zealand . the role botanic gardens play in conservation has become more prominent, with the focus today more on conservation rather than collection
